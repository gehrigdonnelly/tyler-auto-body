What We Fix

Every job done right

From minor dings to major collision damage — Tyler handles it all with the same care and attention to detail.

🚗
Collision Repair

Been in an accident? Tyler will assess the damage, handle the repairs, and restore your vehicle to pre-accident condition. Quality work that lasts.

🔨
Dent & Ding Removal

From parking lot dings to larger dents — precise dent removal that brings your panel back to its original shape without unnecessary filler or painting.

🎨
Paint & Refinishing

Expert color matching and professional refinishing. Whether it's a small chip or a full panel repaint, the finish will be seamless and factory-quality.

🔩
Frame Straightening

Structural damage needs to be fixed right. Precise frame and unibody straightening to restore your vehicle's integrity and keep you safe on the road.

🛡️
Rust Repair

Maine winters are tough on vehicles. Tyler handles rust repair before it spreads — cutting it out properly and patching it the right way so it doesn't come back.

📋
Free Estimates

Not sure what the repair will cost? Stop in or give Tyler a call. Free, honest estimates with no pressure — just a straight answer about what your car needs.
